Q-001

How much does a website cost in India?

+

Our rates are published: a starter one-pager is ₹18,000, a custom landing page ₹35,000, a 5–12 page business website ₹85,000, and an e-commerce store ₹1,75,000 (all excl. GST). Every price is listed on this page before you talk to us.

Q-002

How much does it cost to build a web or mobile app?

+

A starter app (one module, one role) is ₹1,25,000. A full business web app with auth, roles and reporting is ₹4,50,000, and an offline-capable native Android app is ₹2,50,000 — fixed scope, in writing, excl. GST.

Q-003

How long does it take to build a website or app?

+

A starter one-pager ships in 3 working days. A typical custom build runs about six weeks: written spec, build in small reversible steps, independent review, then verification in the real running app before launch.

Q-004

What does the development process look like?

+

Every build starts as a written spec with success criteria. We build in small reversible steps, pass independent quality and security review gates, verify the software live in production, then operate it 24/7 — hosting, monitoring and maintenance from month one.

Q-005

What is managed hosting and why does my website need it?

+

Managed hosting means we host, monitor, patch and back up your software after launch, so downtime is never your problem. Plans start at ₹1,500/month, and every build includes managed operations from month one — software nobody runs stops working.

Q-006

What happens after my website or app is launched?

+

We don't hand over code and vanish. Wednesday hosts and operates what we ship: uptime monitoring, security patches, backups and support under a managed-operations plan, with change requests handled at a published hourly rate of ₹2,500.

Q-007

What is ISO 27001 and does my business need it?

+

ISO 27001 is the international standard for information-security management. You need it when enterprise deals stall on security questionnaires. Our ISO 27001:2022 services are delivered by a certified Lead Auditor — from a ₹35,000 half-day health-check to certification-ready implementation from ₹3,50,000.

Q-008

Do you work with international clients, and what are the payment terms?

+

Yes — we work worldwide; international rates are quoted in USD on request. Terms are the same for everyone: 50% advance before work begins, fixed written scope, balance on delivery, and 15-day payment terms.
